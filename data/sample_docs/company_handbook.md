# Acme Robotics — employee handbook (excerpt)

## PTO policy

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year. PTO
accrues monthly and may be carried into the next calendar year up to a
cap of 30 days. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in
advance via the PTO portal. Sick days are tracked separately and do
not count against PTO.

## Remote work

Acme is a remote-first company. Employees may work from anywhere
within their country of hire. Equipment stipends of $1,500 are
provided in the first month and refreshed every three years.

## Expense reimbursement

Expenses under $50 may be submitted without a receipt. Anything above
that threshold requires either a receipt or a screenshot of the
transaction. Reimbursements are paid out on the next payroll cycle.
Submit through the expenses portal and tag with the relevant project
code.

## Refund policy (for support bot)

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds
above $100 require manager approval and should be escalated to a
human agent. Refunds for clearly fraudulent transactions should be
escalated immediately regardless of amount.
